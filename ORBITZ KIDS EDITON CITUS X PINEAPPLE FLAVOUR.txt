Ingredients:

    1 cup pineapple juice
    1/2 cup orange juice
    1/2 cup lemon-lime soda
    1/2 cup sparkling water
    1/4 cup tapioca pearls (boba balls)
    1-2 tablespoons sugar (optional, to taste)
    Ice cubes
    Fresh pineapple slices and orange slices for garnish

Instructions:

    Prepare the boba balls by soaking them in hot water for about 10-15 minutes, or until they become soft and chewy. Drain and rinse with cold water. Set aside.
    In a large pitcher, combine the pineapple juice, orange juice, and lemon-lime soda. Stir well.
    Add the sparkling water to the pitcher and stir gently to combine.
    Taste the mixture and add sugar if you prefer a sweeter drink. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
    Fill glasses with ice cubes.
    Divide the boba balls evenly among the glasses.
    Pour the citrus pineapple mixture over the boba balls, filling each glass about 3/4 full.
    Garnish with fresh pineapple slices and orange slices.
    Serve immediately with a wide straw to enjoy the boba balls.

Choose a heating element: You can use a ceramic or quartz rod as your heating element. These materials are known for their ability to heat up quickly and evenly, providing a consistent vaping experience.

Find a suitable container: You can use a glass tube or tank as your container. Make sure it is heat-resistant and can hold your chosen e-liquid.

Connect the heating element to the USB port: You will need to solder the heating element to a wire that can be plugged into a USB port. Make sure the connection is secure and does not overheat.

Attach the container to the heating element: Use silicone or heat-resistant tape to attach the glass tube or tank to the heating element. This will ensure that the e-liquid comes into contact with the heating element when it is powered on.

Fill the container with e-liquid: Be careful not to overfill the container, as this can cause leaks. Use a syringe or dropper to add your chosen e-liquid to the glass tube or tank.

Test the vape: Plug the vape into a USB port and turn it on. Inhale gently on the mouthpiece to draw vapor into your mouth. Adjust the power settings as needed to achieve your desired level of vapor production.

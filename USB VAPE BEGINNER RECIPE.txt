🔧 STEP-BY-STEP BUILD
Step 1 — Build the coil (do this first, it's the heart)
Wrap 5 turns of 26 AWG kanthal around a 3mm nail or dowel.
Trim legs to ~15mm each, bend them flat and parallel.
Measure resistance with your multimeter — target 1.0–1.5 Ω. Too low? Add a turn. Too high? Remove one.
Soak a small pad of cotton (or fold ceramic fiber) and wrap it through the coil center.
Thread the coil legs through two holes drilled in a small stainless disc (or bend a U-shape from stainless sheet) — this becomes the coil mount.
Test before assembly: Dip the wick in your juice, hold the coil over a cup, and power it briefly from the buck module. It should glow faintly orange within seconds and produce gentle vapor. If it smells burnt immediately, your voltage is too high — dial the buck down.

Step 2 — Wire the electronics
On a breadboard first:
Buck module VIN+ / VIN− → USB-C 5V / GND
Buck VOUT+ → one leg of MOSFET drain
MOSFET source → coil leg A
Coil leg B → USB GND (common return)
Button between MOSFET gate and ground, with 10kΩ pull-up to VOUT+
Verify with multimeter: pressing the button should drop the voltage across the MOSFET to near 0V (coil gets full power); releasing restores it.
Once confirmed, transfer to a small perfboard or solder directly into the pen shell.
Step 3 — Assemble the pen
Mount the buck module + MOSFET board at the bottom of the pen shell (heaviest part lowest = balanced feel).
Route the USB-C connector out the bottom cap.
Place the glass tube tank in the middle section, secured by the metal sleeve.
Insert the coil mount so the coil sits inside the glass tube, centered, not touching the walls.
Attach the drip tip on top.
Run coil wires up through the tank base plate, solder to terminals that press against the coil legs (a simple spring-loaded contact works).
Step 4 — Juice it (your step, not mine)
Fill the glass tube ~⅔ with your Citrus Fizz juice.
Let the wick soak 10 minutes before first puff.
First 3 puffs: short hits, watch for dry spots.

Known limitations (honest ones):

You're tethered to wherever the USB port is. Desk-vaping device, basically.
No variable wattage — you get what the buck outputs. Fix: swap in a PWM dimmer chip (NE555 timer driving the MOSFET) for 2–3 fixed power levels. Fun weekend upgrade.
Glass tube must be heat-rated. Borosilicate (Pyrex-type) is ideal.
🛠️ UPGRADE PATH (once v1 works)
PWM power control — NE555 + potentiometer = 3 power settings on the button (short/medium/long press).
Temp sensor — Thermistor in the tank wall + Arduino Nano = auto shut-off if coil overheats. Safety net worth building.
LED indicator — Small RGB LED showing "ready / firing / coil hot."
Magnetic quick-release tank — Two neodymium magnets let you pop the tank off for cleaning without unscrewing.
Enclosure redesign — 3D-print a proper pen casing with a hidden USB pocket and a sliding tank window.


A large capacitor (at least 1000uF)
A 9V battery
A push button switch
Two wires (at least 18 inches long)
Here are the steps to follow:

Remove the top of the capacitor by unscrewing it or using a knife to cut the insulation. Be careful not to touch the metal parts of the capacitor with your bare hands.

Connect one wire to the capacitor's metal case and the other wire to the push button switch's metal prong.

Connect the other side of the switch to the battery's positive terminal.

Connect the other end of the capacitor to the battery's negative terminal.

When you press the button, it will complete the circuit, allowing electricity to flow from the battery through the capacitor, creating a large electrical discharge. This discharge can be loud and potentially harmful, so use caution when operating this device.

Make sure the capacitor is properly rated for the voltage and current you are using. Never attempt to use this device with higher voltages or currents than recommended.

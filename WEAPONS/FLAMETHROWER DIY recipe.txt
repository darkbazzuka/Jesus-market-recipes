Materials:

    Hot glue gun and glue sticks
    Wooden stick (around 12 inches long and 1/2 inch thick)
    Candle (any size, with a removable base)
    Lighter or matches

Steps:

    Prepare the wooden stick:
        Ensure the wooden stick is clean and free of any debris.

    Create a candle holder:
        Apply hot glue to the wooden stick, creating a holder for the candle.
        Shape the glue to form a secure and stable base for the candle.
        Allow the glue to cool and harden completely.

    Design the swappable feature:
        Create a small indentation in the glue holder using a hot glue stick or a similar tool.
        This indentation will allow the candle to be easily removed and replaced.

    Test the flame thrower:
        Light the candle using a lighter or matches.
        Hold the flame thrower by the wooden stick and test its functionality.
        Ensure the flame is consistent and controllable.

    Swap the candle:
        Once the candle has burned down, carefully remove it from the holder.
        Insert a new candle into the holder, ensuring it is secure and stable.

    Practice:
        Use the flame thrower to practice and improve your technique.
        Be cautious and keep a safe distance from flammable materials.

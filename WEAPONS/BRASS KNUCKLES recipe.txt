Materials:

    1/4" thick brass sheet (or other strong metal)
    Metal saw or laser cutter
    Sandpaper (80, 120, 220, 400, 600 grit)
    Metal files
    Drill and 1/8" drill bit
    1/8" diameter steel rod (or other strong metal)
    Soldering iron and solder
    Polishing cloth

Steps:

    Design
        Create a brass knuckles template with 4-5 finger holes, a palm grip, and a knuckle guard.
        The knuckle guard should be slightly curved to fit the hand.

    Cut
        Use a metal saw or laser cutter to cut the brass sheet according to the template.
        Ensure the edges are smooth.

    Sand
        Sand the brass knuckles with 80 grit sandpaper to remove any burrs or sharp edges.
        Gradually move to finer grits (120, 220, 400, 600) for a smooth finish.

    File
        Use metal files to refine the shape and smooth any rough spots.
        Pay special attention to the finger holes and knuckle guard.

    Drill
        Drill 1/8" holes in the finger holes and the palm grip.
        Ensure the holes are straight and smooth.

    Rod
        Cut a 1/8" diameter steel rod to the desired length (around 2-3 inches).
        This will be the knuckle guard.

    Solder
        Solder the steel rod to the knuckle guard, ensuring it is secure and straight.

    Polish
        Use a polishing cloth to give the brass knuckles a shiny finish.

    Test
        Try on the brass knuckles and ensure they fit comfortably and securely.

    Practice
        Use the brass knuckles to practice strikes and improve your technique.

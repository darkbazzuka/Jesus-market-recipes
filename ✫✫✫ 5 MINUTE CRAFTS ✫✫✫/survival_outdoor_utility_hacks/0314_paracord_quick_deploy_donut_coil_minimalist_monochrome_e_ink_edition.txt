6. ENGINEERING & PHYSICS PRINCIPLES BEHIND THIS CRAFT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Even a 5-minute household craft relies on real-world mechanical engineering:
  * Geometric Rigidity: Flat sheets bend easily under perpendicular force, but
    introducing a 19-degree fold or triangular gusset increases the area
    moment of inertia dramatically without adding extra weight.
  * Friction & Normal Force: Mechanical joints hold without glue when opposing
    surfaces generate sufficient normal force across a high-friction interface.
  * Elastic Energy Storage: Materials like spring steel clips, rubber bands, and
    corrugated fibers store recoverable strain energy when deflected within their
    elastic limit.

             fibers with a thin strip of matte tape.

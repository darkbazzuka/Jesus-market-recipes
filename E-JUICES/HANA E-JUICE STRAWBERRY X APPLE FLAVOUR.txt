Strawberry-Apple E-Juice (30ml bottle, 70/30 VG/PG)


Ingredients & Supplies

Flavor concentrates (all water-soluble, PG-based):

    1.2ml TFA Strawberry (Ripe)
    0.6ml Capella Double Apple
    0.6ml Capella Strawberry Sweet
    0.6ml Flavorah/FA Fuji Apple
    0.3ml TFA Sweetener

Base liquids:

    ~19ml Vegetable Glycerin (VG) — USP grade
    ~5ml Propylene Glycol (PG) — USP grade (flavorings already add ~3.3ml PG)
    Nicotine base (optional) — e.g., if using 36mg/ml PG-based nic and you want 3mg, that's 2.5ml; subtract that amount from your PG

Equipment:

    One 30ml glass or PET bottle with cap
    Syringes (1ml for flavorings, 10ml+ for base) or a scale accurate to 0.01g (mixing by weight is more precise)
    Nitrile gloves
    Label + marker
    Paper towels

Steps

    1.

    Clean your workspace. Wipe down, wash hands, put on gloves. Nicotine absorbs through skin, so gloves are non-negotiable if you're using it.
    2.

    Lay out and label. Line up your concentrates and write the recipe on your bottle label before you start mixing — inky fingerprints on a full bottle are annoying.
    3.

    Add flavorings first. Using separate syringes (or rinse between each), pull and add to the bottle:
        1.2ml Strawberry Ripe
        0.6ml Double Apple
        0.6ml Strawberry Sweet
        0.6ml Fuji Apple
        0.3ml Sweetener
    4.

    Add nicotine (if using). Draw your calculated amount slowly and add it to the bottle. Cap and set the empty syringe somewhere safe to rinse.
    5.

    Top off with PG, then VG. Add enough PG to bring total PG (including flavors) to 30% of the bottle, then fill nearly to 30ml with VG. VG is thick — pull it into the syringe slowly and let air bubbles escape before reading the volume.
    6.

    Cap and shake hard for a solid 60 seconds. The mix will look slightly cloudy at first, then uniform.
    7.

    Steep. Store the bottle in a cool, dark place (a drawer works) with the cap on. Shake once or twice daily. Taste-test after day 3, but it really hits its stride around day 7 — strawberries need time to bloom, and the apple will fade slightly as they do.
    8.

    Test before committing. Drip a small amount or use a cheap, replaceable coil — sweetener gunks coils, so save your good ones for mixes you've confirmed you love.

Quick tips

    Everything is USP grade and from a reputable vendor — never use grocery-store flavoring extracts with oil in them (oils are dangerous to inhale).
    Keep notes: date, exact percentages, and taste impressions at days 1, 3, 7, and 14. Your future self will thank you when you want to tweak it.
    If strawberry reads too weak after steeping, bump Strawberry Ripe to 5% next batch. If the apple tastes like black licorice, that's the Double Apple — swap half of it for more Fuji.

Shake the finished bottle one last time, fill your tank, and take that first test puff slow.

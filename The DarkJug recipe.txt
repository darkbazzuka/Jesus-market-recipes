Ingredients (The Fume-Lock Formula)
1 cup (200 g) white granulated sugar

1/2 cup pure Everclear (190 proof / 95% ABV) or high-proof spirit (The higher the proof, the more aggressive the vapor punch)

3 tablespoons pure food-grade quinine powder

2 tablespoons high-proof absinthe (adds a volatile wall of wormwood, anise, and heavy herbal essential oils that cling to the vapors)

A tight-sealing glass mason jar (essential for trapping the volatile fumes)

Instructions
Mix the Liquid Weapon: In a small bowl, thoroughly whisk together the Everclear, absinthe, and quinine powder until the quinine is fully dissolved into a potent, bitter spirit blend.

Submerge the Sugar: Place your granulated sugar into the glass mason jar. Pour the high-proof bitter mixture directly over the sugar.

Do Not Bake (The Cold-Soak Method): Screw the lid on tightly. Do not put this in the oven—heat destroys the volatile alcohol fumes you want to capture. Instead, let the jar sit at room temperature for 48 hours, shaking it vigorously every few hours.

The Absorption Matrix: The sugar will absorb a massive amount of the high-proof liquid, forming a dense, semi-liquid slurry or a wet crystalline mash saturated with unevaporated 95% alcohol.

The Fume Trap: Because the jar is sealed, the high-proof alcohol and volatile herbal oils continuously off-gas into the headspace of the container.

The Result
When you unscrew the lid of the jar, a dense, stinging cloud of pure high-proof ethanol vapor mixed with heavy herbal bitterness billows out. Just cracking the container open fills the immediate air with the intense smell of a heavy bar drink, while the wet sugar crystals inside remain completely saturated with a maximum-potency bitter punch.

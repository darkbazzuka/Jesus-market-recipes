👅 WHY THIS IS ADDICTIVE (the science part)
Nicotine salt at 20 mg — fast absorption, smooth delivery, no harshness, hits faster than freebase. That's the hook.
Citric acid — real sourness triggers salivation. Salivation = craving signal. Your brain starts prepping for the next puff.
Coolant (WS-23) — activates TRPM8 cold receptors without minty flavor. Feels like biting into ice. Refreshing ≠ minty, and it doesn't mask the citrus.
Soda note — mimics carbonation. Fizz is associated with reward/dopamine even though there's no CO2. It tricks your palate.
Sweet-sour balance — sugar + acid is the most craveable flavor pairing in food science. This replicates it in vapor form.
🥤 STEP-BY-STEP
Sanitize everything. Rinse bottle with a splash of 100% PG, discard.
Weigh your base into the bottle first (biggest volume, least error).
Add nicotine last-to-base order matters less, but adding it now keeps flavor dosing clean.
Add flavors in this order: lemon → lime → soda → malt/vanilla → coolant.
Dissolve citric acid separately: 0.5 g citric acid + 5 ml warm water, stir till clear. Add 0.5–1 ml of that solution. (Never add dry powder directly — it clumps.)
Cap and shake hard for 30 seconds. Tap bubbles down.
Steep. Minimum 24 hours, ideally 3–5 days, at room temperature, capped, shaken once daily. Citric acid needs time to mellow from "battery acid" to "bright tart."
Taste-test with a cotton swab (not your tongue — nicotine!) after day 2. Adjust:
Too flat? +0.2 ml citric solution
Too sharp? +0.2 ml sweetener/malt
Not fizzy enough? +0.3 ml soda flavor
Final check: should taste like biting a cold lemon-lime soda on a hot day. If yes — done.
❄️ REFRESHING BOOSTERS (optional upgrades)
Dry ice steep: drop a tiny piece of dry ice (wrapped in paper towel) into the bottle for 10 minutes while shaking. Intense chill effect. Don't overdo it.
Menthol-free coolant > menthol. Menthol tastes like toothpaste; WS-23 tastes like cold.
Refrigerate the finished juice. Cold juice = extra refreshment perception.
Ice cube trick: some vapers keep their bottle in a mini-fridge. Works
